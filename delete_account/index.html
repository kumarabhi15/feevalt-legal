Delete your FeeValt account

You can delete your account here without installing the app. Sign in with the Google account you use for FeeValt, and we will remove it straight away. This cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in — your FeeValt account is removed and you will not be able to sign in with it again.
  • Your reward email — the address any referral voucher would have been sent to.

The data on your phone is not cleared by deleting your account here. Your subscriptions, recharge plans, savings history and settings stay on the phone as they are. The next time you open FeeValt, it carries on with a new account that is not linked to your Google account, still holding that same data. To clear what's on your phone too, open FeeValt and use Settings → Delete all my Data, or uninstall the app.

What we keep, and why

  • Referral, purchase and reward records, for up to 90 days — kept after your account is deleted, primarily so we can investigate potential abuse of the referral and reward system, then permanently deleted. They can no longer be linked back to a sign-in once your account is gone.
  • A referral whose reward is still unpaid — if you were part of a referral where a reward to the other person hasn't yet been paid or refunded, the record of that referral may be kept slightly longer than 90 days, until that reward reaches a final state.
  • Reward vouchers paid out through your account — not deleted outright. We keep the amount, date and status for our own bookkeeping, with the link to your account removed after 90 days.
  • Purchase history held by Google Play. Deleting your FeeValt account does not cancel a purchase or request a refund — for that, contact Google Play.

We only use this to confirm it is your account.
